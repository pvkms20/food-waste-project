How to read these numbers

Predicted waste is the model's estimate of food that will be left over. The shaded bar is a {{ 90 }}% range based on the model's past errors, so the real figure will usually fall inside it.

Estimated edible surplus is the predicted waste multiplied by the share you said is still safe. Suggested to offer is that surplus, limited by the capacity of the matched NGO.

Risk bands: low under {{ '%g'|format(rules.low) }} kg, medium up to {{ '%g'|format(rules.medium) }} kg, high above. The quantities actually offered, collected and delivered are recorded later on the donation itself.
