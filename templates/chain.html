How it works

Each record gets a fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) made from its contents and the previous record's fingerprint. Changing any past record changes its fingerprint, which no longer matches the next record, so the edit is exposed.

What it does not do

This is a learning simulation on one computer: no network, no mining, no consensus. It detects edits to individual records but cannot prevent someone with full database access from rewriting the whole book.
